Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 11 March 2026

Zero Fleets ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the Zero Fleets mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Aadhaar photo (for identity verification)
  • Driving licence image
  • Selfie image

Operational Information

  • Duty records and working hours
  • Trip earnings and expenses
  • Vehicle information and number plate
  • Business UPI or banking name for payments

Device Information

  • Device model
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Crash diagnostics and performance data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
  • Create and manage your account
  • Allow drivers to connect with fleet owners
  • Allow fleet owners to manage vehicles and drivers
  • Record duties, earnings, and work history
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Send service notifications and updates
  • Provide customer support

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Earnings Screenshot Processing

Zero Fleets allows drivers to upload earnings screenshots during duty sessions. These screenshots are stored securely and may be processed using automated image analysis to extract earnings values.

  • The uploaded screenshot is stored securely in our cloud storage.
  • The screenshot may be processed using automated image analysis to identify earnings information.
  • This processing occurs only when a user chooses to upload a screenshot.
  • The screenshot is not used for AI training.
Users may alternatively choose to manually enter earnings instead of uploading a screenshot.

4. Data Storage

Zero Fleets uses cloud infrastructure provided by Google Firebase services, including:
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore database
  • Firebase Cloud Storage
These services are operated by Google LLC and use industry-standard security practices to protect stored data.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate the application.

Firebase (Google LLC)

Firebase provides authentication, cloud database storage, and file storage used by the app. More information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Image Analysis Service

When drivers upload earnings screenshots for automatic earnings extraction, the image may be securely processed by a third-party image analysis service to extract earnings values. The image is used only for this feature and is not stored for AI training.

6. Data Sharing

We may share information only in the following circumstances:
  • Between drivers and fleet owners as required for the app’s functionality
  • With service providers such as Firebase for hosting and infrastructure
  • When required by law or legal authorities

7. Data Retention

We retain user data for as long as the account remains active or as needed to provide our services. If you request account deletion, your personal information will be removed or anonymized within a reasonable time unless required for legal compliance.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

You may:
  • Access or update your personal information within the app
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing
To request deletion or data access, contact us at: zerofleets.in@gmail.com

Zero Fleets is intended for professional drivers and fleet operators and is not designed for individuals under 18 years of age.
