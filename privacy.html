Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zero Fleets ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information. By using our app, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve our services:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Aadhaar photo
  • Driving licence image
  • Selfie image

Usage Information

  • Duty records (trip data, working hours, earnings)
  • Vehicle number plate
  • Banking name, Business UPI
  • Device information (model, operating system, app version)

Payment & Subscription Information

For paid features, we use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect:

  • Purchase history and subscription status
  • App user ID and device identifiers
  • Transaction IDs from the App Store or Google Play
Note: We do not directly collect or store your payment card details. All payment transactions are processed securely by Apple App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat only receives transaction metadata, not your full payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Help fleet owners manage their vehicles and drivers
  • Help drivers connect with fleets and log duties
  • Manage and verify your subscription or in-app purchase status
  • Unlock paid features based on your active subscription
  • Maintain app performance and improve user experience
  • Communicate updates, support, and service notifications

We do not sell or rent user data to anyone.

3. Data Storage

All data is securely stored using Firebase services (Firestore, Authentication, and Storage). Firebase is a Google product with strong industry-grade security and encryption standards.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Zero Fleets:

Firebase (Google)

Used for authentication, database storage, and file storage. Firebase processes data in accordance with Google's privacy standards. Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat acts as a data processor on our behalf and may collect the following to deliver subscription management services:

  • App user ID and anonymous device identifiers
  • Purchase and subscription transaction data from Apple or Google
  • Subscription status, entitlements, and renewal information

RevenueCat does not receive your name, payment card details, or government-issued documents. Their data handling is governed by their own Privacy Policy, available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple App Store / Google Play

All payment transactions for in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google directly. We do not have access to your payment card or billing information. Their respective privacy policies govern how payment data is handled.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share your data only in the following cases:

  • With fleet owners or drivers as part of app functionality (e.g., when connecting a driver with a fleet)
  • With RevenueCat to validate and manage your subscription entitlements
  • When required by law enforcement or government authorities
  • With service providers (like Firebase) for technical support and data storage

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Zero Fleets offers paid features through subscriptions and one-time purchases managed via RevenueCat. By making a purchase, you agree to the terms of your respective app store (Apple App Store or Google Play Store).

  • Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled before the renewal date
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your App Store or Google Play account settings
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google in accordance with their respective policies
  • Access to paid features will continue until the end of the current billing period after cancellation

7. Security

We take all reasonable steps to protect your data using modern security practices. However, no online system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.
