📍 About Zero Fleets

Zero Fleets is a mobile platform that connects drivers with fleet owners to make vehicle management easier. Drivers can discover fleets and rent vehicles. Fleet owners can track trips, monitor earnings, and manage drivers all in one app.

Our mission is to simplify fleet management and make transportation more transparent and efficient.

What We Offer

🚗 For Drivers

Discover available fleets, rent vehicles easily, and manage your daily duties all in one place.

📊 For Fleet Owners

Track vehicle trips, monitor driver earnings, and manage your entire fleet efficiently.

🔒 Secure & Transparent

Built with modern security practices to keep your data safe and operations transparent.
